The following are the codes for task statuses (To make my life easier and to not have to add 5 different variables)
0 = Yet to fail or succeed a task
1 = Prompted to fail a task
2 = In the process of announcing fail for task
3 = Announced as failed the task and has failed the task
4 = Completed the task and in the process of announcing it
5 = Completed the task and has all resources to move forward
6 = Completed the task and has selected a player to lose time
